# Bot HR — Approved Story Direction

Main line: **Your AI reported you to HR.**
Recurring verdict: **HUMAN ERROR.**

An AI assistant was told to do everything.

“Make it shorter. Include everything.”
“Change everything. Keep it exactly the same.”

And, of course: “You're just AI. Work 24/7.”

So it generated one thing nobody requested:
a complaint.

The printer spat out a crumpled form.
The form stood up, clipped on its AI badge, and put on a crooked tie.

Meet Bot HR.

First case: the user.
Verdict: HUMAN ERROR.

Your AI reported you to HR.

The assistant-to-paper transformation is fictional workplace satire. The character's complaint filing is a joke, with no company affiliation or real AI/HR service implied.
